2. Remember

🔴 Centre

  • The fixed point in the middle of a circle is centre of the circle.
  • Every circle has only one center point.

🟠 Radius

  • The line connecting the center to any point on the circle is radius.
  • We can draw an infinite number of radii (plural of radius) in a circle.
  • All radii of a circle are equal in length.

🟢 Diameter

  • A straight line passing through the center that connects two points on the circle.
  • It is the longest straight line that can be drawn inside a circle.
  • It divides the circle into two equal halves (semicircles).

🟣 Circumference

  • The boundary (outer edge) of the circle is its circumference.
  • Its length is the distance all the way around the circle.

🎯 Same centre

  • Circles drawn with different radii using the same center point.
  • These are called concentric circles.

✨ Magic rule

Diameter = 2 × Radius
Radius = Diameter ÷ 2

6. Detective time: find the centre! 🕵️

You drew a circle around a bangle or a lid. Where is its centre? What is its radius? Try these tricks.

  1. Put the square corner of a paper on the edge of the circle.
  2. The two sides of the paper cut the circle at A and B.
  3. Line AB is always a diameter! Mark it.
  4. Move the corner to another place. Mark a second line.
  5. The lines cross at the centre. 🎯
