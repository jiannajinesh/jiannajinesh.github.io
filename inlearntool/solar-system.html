3. How heavy would you be? ⚖️

Each planet pulls with a different force (gravity). Type your weight on Earth:

kg on Earth 🌍

4. Remember 🧠

☀️ The SunA star at the centre. It gives light and heat to all planets.
🌀 OrbitThe path a planet takes around the Sun.
🪨 Rocky planetsMercury, Venus, Earth, Mars: small and made of rock.
🎈 Giant planetsJupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune: huge and made mostly of gas and ice.
🌙 MoonA natural object that goes around a planet. Earth has 1 Moon.
🔄 Day & yearA day = one spin. A year = one trip around the Sun.
🧊 PlutoSince 2006, Pluto is called a dwarf planet.
